# Privacy

Echo collects nothing.

## What leaves your device

Nothing. This app has no internet access.

- **Your voice.** Each take is recorded into memory, compressed and played back on the phone. It is never written to storage and never sent anywhere.
- **Backups.** Echo keeps no data, and is excluded from cloud backups and phone-to-phone transfers.
- **Links.** Links in About open in your browser or mail app, only when you tap them. Echo itself never connects to anything.

The same list is in the app, under About, "What leaves your device".

## What stays on the phone

- The last take, in memory only, until you leave the app.
- During a test, the phone is in call mode on your headset. Echo restores the previous audio mode when the test ends.

## Erasing

There is nothing to erase: leaving the app clears the take, and uninstalling removes the app.
