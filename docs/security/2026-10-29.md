# Security review, 24 Oct 2026

Internal review of Clipshot 1.3.0 (commit tagged `v1.3.0`). No external audit yet.

## Scope

Permissions, network, storage, crypto, exported components, the Capture accessibility service, release signing and CI.

## Findings

**Permissions.** The merged release manifests ask for exactly the permissions in [`docs/permissions.txt`](../permissions.txt): read images (or read storage on Android 11 and 12), start at boot, All files access (full flavor only, optional, asked when "Remove from gallery" is turned on), and the signature-level `DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION` that AndroidX declares for itself. CI now fails on any permission outside that list.

**Network.** No INTERNET permission in either flavor, so the app cannot open a socket. The links added to About (repository, release notes, private vulnerability reporting, privacy policy, e-mail) are handed to the browser or the mail app with an explicit tap; the app itself sends nothing.

**Storage.** Settings and counters live in one private SharedPreferences file. The last three screenshots are copied into `noBackupFilesDir/shots`, served read-only by `ShotProvider`, which is not exported, accepts only `shot-<digits>.<png|jpg|webp>` names and is reached only through the clipboard's one-off grant. Backups and device-to-device transfers are disabled (`allowBackup=false`, `dataExtractionRules`). Nothing is written to shared storage.

**Crypto.** None is needed: no account, no key, no data leaves the phone. The only hash is the SHA-256 of the installed signing certificate, computed for display in About.

**Exported components.** `MainActivity` (launcher) and the two Quick Settings tiles, protected by `BIND_QUICK_SETTINGS_TILE`. Everything else (`AboutActivity`, `CaptureSetupActivity`, jobs, provider, boot receiver) is not exported. `AboutActivity` reads one boolean extra.

**Capture service** (full flavor). Subscribes to no accessibility event, `canRetrieveWindowContent="false"`, no gestures, no key or fingerprint filtering, one flag for the accessibility button. Its calls are `GLOBAL_ACTION_DISMISS_NOTIFICATION_SHADE` and `GLOBAL_ACTION_TAKE_SCREENSHOT`, plus `disableSelf()` from "Erase everything". `CaptureConfigTest` guards the declaration; the play flavor removes it and CI checks the merged manifest.

**Gallery removal** (full flavor, optional). Deletes only rows owned by a system screenshot app, re-checked (path, owner, dates) just before deleting, one minute after the copy.

**Signing.** One release key, kept outside the repository by the developer. Certificate SHA-256 published in `SECURITY.md` and, from 1.3.0, shown in About from the installed APK so users can compare.

## Fixed in this release

- Added "Erase everything": settings, counters, private copies, pending gallery removals and Clipshot's clip on the clipboard are deleted, gallery removal is turned off and the Capture service turns itself off.
- Added the About screen (signing certificate read at run time, review date, private reporting link) and "What leaves your device".
- CI: permission allowlist, review age (6 months), changelog entry for the version, and a repository scan for personal paths and forbidden text.

## Remaining

- The clipboard is readable by the focused app and the keyboard, as on any Android phone. Clipshot cannot change that.
- The private copies are not encrypted at rest; they rely on Android's file-based encryption and app sandbox, like the gallery they come from.
- The signing key is a file, not a hardware key. Moving it to hardware is planned.
- No external audit.

Next review: before 24 Apr 2027, or at any change to permissions, storage or the Capture service.
